## App name

**Safe75 — Safe 75%**

## Tagline / Short description

Track, simulate, and plan your class attendance to stay safely above your institution's threshold — offline-first and privacy-focused.

## Full description (long)

**Safe75** helps university and college students keep their attendance
above the minimum required percentage with a clean, offline-first experience.

**Key features**

- **Attendance dashboard** — see your overall and per-subject percentages at a
  glance, with safe / warning / critical status colors.
- **Weekly timetable** — add subjects, class slots, rooms, and teachers; the
  schedule screen maps your whole week.
- **Smart reminders** — a morning summary of today's classes, post-class
  "did you attend?" notifications with one-tap Present / Absent / Cancelled
  actions, and an end-of-day nudge for anything you missed.
- **Timetable OCR import** — photograph a printed or on-screen timetable and
  have subjects, times, and rooms extracted locally — nothing leaves your device.
- **Predictive simulator** — see how skipping or attending future classes
  changes your percentage before you make the call.
- **Leave planner** — check the impact of planned leaves against your targets.
- **Home-screen widget** — your attendance percentage, today's progress, and the
  next class right on your home screen, with quick actions.
- **Full data control** — export a JSON backup, restore selectively, archive a
  finished semester, reset for a new one, and run a data-integrity scan.
- **Privacy-focused reporting** — attendance data stays on your device. If you
  explicitly submit a bug report, only the description, technical diagnostics,
  app/device metadata, and optional screenshot are uploaded. No accounts and no
  analytics.
