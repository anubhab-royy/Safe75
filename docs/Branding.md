# App Branding Document

This document establishes the app's visual identity, app icons, color psychology, and Play Store metadata specifications for **Attendance Tracker**.

---

## 1. Visual Brand Guidelines

### 1.1 Application Name
- **Primary Name:** Attendance Tracker
- **Tagline:** Safe at 75%
- **Brand Style:** Clean, minimalist, and educational, yet highly tool-oriented.

### 1.2 Color Psychology
- **Deep Blue / Indigo:** The primary color represents reliability, structure, and academic success. It inspires a sense of calm and organization.
- **Teal / Pastel Green:** Used for the "Safe" attendance compliance status, invoking a feeling of safety, progress, and tranquility.
- **Warm Orange:** Serves as a warning status, encouraging users to attend classes without triggering panic.
- **Muted Gray-Indigo Backgrounds:** Provide a premium dark mode layout, reducing eye fatigue during late-night studies.

---

## 2. Logo & App Icon Concepts

### 2.1 Logo Concept
The logo consists of a circular progress ring integrated with a calendar grid.
- **Symbol:** A stylized calendar page where the calendar grid transforms into a circular progress meter.
- **Text:** "Attendance Tracker" rendered in custom sans-serif typography.

### 2.2 Adaptive App Icon Structure
For Android 8.0+ devices:
- **Foreground Layer:** A clean calendar card containing an active green checkmark and a circular 75% progress loop.
- **Background Layer:** A deep blue gradient matching the Material 3 color system.
- **Monochrome Version:** A simplified calendar checkmark outline compatible with Material You themed icon adaptive launchers.

---

## 3. Splash Screen Specifications

The splash screen is built using the AndroidX Splash Screen API:
1. **Background Color:** `#121212` in Dark Mode, `#FFFFFF` in Light Mode.
2. **Foreground Icon:** The vector logo checkmark.
3. **Transition Animation:** The progress ring animates up to 75% before fading out to the onboarding layout.

## 4. Play Store Identity Copy

### 4.1 Short Description (80 Characters Max)
> Easily track your class attendance, simulate skip impacts, and meet targets.

### 4.2 Full Description
> Take control of your academic schedule with Attendance Tracker!
> 
> Enforcing strict 75% university attendance rules can be stressful. Attendance Tracker helps you stay organized. With predictive analytics, daily logging, and timetable schedules, you always know where you stand.
> 
> KEY FEATURES:
> - **Timetable OCR Import:** Take a photo of your schedule to import classes in seconds.
> - **Skip Simulator:** Predict how missing a class impacts your eligibility before you skip.
> - **Leave Planner:** Check timing risks before blocking leave dates.
> - **100% Offline-First:** Your data belongs to you. Zero tracking, zero ads.
